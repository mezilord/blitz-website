Our Guarantee

Last updated November 18, 2026

We want you to feel confident hiring us. These are the promises we make on every project, and how they work in practice.

We finish what we start

We don't stop until the work in your proposal is complete and you're happy with it. If something isn't right, tell us and we'll fix it.

On-time delivery

Your proposal includes a timeline, and we plan our work to meet it. If anything puts a deadline at risk, we'll tell you as soon as we know and agree a new plan with you. Timelines depend on receiving content and feedback on schedule.

Revisions

Every project includes revision rounds. Our Starter website package includes two rounds, and our Professional package includes unlimited revisions within the agreed scope. Your proposal confirms what's included for your project.

Money-back guarantee

If you're not happy with our first designs, tell us before you approve them. We'll revise them, and if we still can't get it right, you can cancel the project and we'll refund what you've paid for work you haven't approved, less any third-party costs already incurred on your behalf.

You own your work

Once your project is paid in full, you own the final deliverables we create for you. See our Terms of Service for details.

Confidentiality

Your ideas and data stay private. We'll sign a non-disclosure agreement before we start if you'd like one.

Support after launch

Website projects include post-launch support, with 30 days on our Professional package. Ongoing maintenance plans are available from $200 per month.

Questions

Email hello@blitz-creative.com and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
