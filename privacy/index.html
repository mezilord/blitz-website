Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Blitz Creative (“we”, “us”) is a design and development studio working with clients in Canada and Bahrain. This policy explains what personal information we collect through blitz-creative.com and our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We only collect what we need to talk with you and deliver your project:

  • Contact details you give us when you book a call or email us, such as your name, email address, phone number and company.
  • Project information you share with us, such as briefs, content, files and feedback.
  • Billing information needed to invoice you. Card payments are handled by our payment provider; we don't store card numbers.
  • Technical information such as your browser type, device and pages visited, which our hosting provider may record in standard server logs.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and schedule calls.
  • To plan, deliver and support your project.
  • To send invoices and keep business records.
  • To keep our website secure and working properly.
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations.

We don't sell your information

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers below, when needed to run our business, or when the law requires it.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the website and our studio, including:

  • Cal.com, to schedule calls you book through our website.
  • Our web hosting and email providers.
  • Payment and accounting providers, for invoices and payments.
  • Design, development and AI tools used to deliver your project, where relevant.

These providers may process information in Canada, Bahrain and other countries, and are expected to protect it to a standard consistent with this policy.

Cookies and embedded content

Our website does not use advertising cookies. Some pages include content from other sites, such as the Cal.com booking calendar and live previews of our clients' websites. Those sites may set their own cookies, which are covered by their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, including any period required for accounting or legal reasons, and then delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Bahrain's Personal Data Protection Law, you can:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Withdraw your consent to how we use it.
  • Complain to your local privacy regulator.

To make a request, email hello@blitz-creative.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, and we sign NDAs with clients on request. No system is completely secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive information by email.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be highlighted on the website.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@blitz-creative.com.
